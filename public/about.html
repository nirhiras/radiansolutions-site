NASA SEWP Program Manager

Niranjan Hiras

President

Niranjan Hiras is the President of Radian Solutions, specializing in IT and management consulting. An alumnus of Osmania University in India and Texas A&M University, his career spans hands-on engineer, software programmer, manager and business owner. He is a visionary, independent thinker and pragmatic leader with an uncanny ability to find solutions to complex problems.

Under his leadership Radian has landed on the Inc. 5000 and Washington Technology Fast 50 lists. His philosophy: “doing it right the first time.”
