// Overview

About this vehicle

The Technology, Digital and Data Consulting IT Master Services Agreement (TDDC IT-MSA) is California’s premier contracting vehicle for technology consulting. Managed by the California Department of Technology (CDT), it lets state agencies procure IT consulting from pre-qualified vendors with standardized rates and terms.

Radian holds a Tier 3 classification, authorizing engagements of up to $5 million per task order. That positions us to support significant IT modernization, cloud migration and digital transformation initiatives for California state agencies.

// How to order

Order through TDDC IT-MSA

California state agencies work through CDT’s MSA process with pre-qualified vendors at standardized rates and terms.
Contact Radian to scope an engagement up to the Tier 3 limit of $5 million per engagement.
