Overview

About this vehicle

The California Multiple Award Schedule (CMAS) provides for the purchase and warranty of highly adaptive cybersecurity services and Information Technology (IT) consulting services. Radian Solutions, LLC holds a non-mandatory CMAS issued by the California Department of General Services, Procurement Division.

CMAS gives California state agencies and local government entities a streamlined way to buy from pre-qualified vendors. Job titles and restrictions applicable to this CMAS are set out in the schedule itself.
